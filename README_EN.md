# DoraAmo

[中文](README.md) | [English](README_EN.md)

A portal mod for Minecraft 1.12.2 (Forge).

Adds post–End City gameplay focused on late-game travel. The Anywhere Door lets you teleport to places you choose.

The Anywhere Door is two blocks tall. Step inside and wait a moment to reach the other side. By default it leads nowhere; you can also switch it to scaled travel between the Overworld and the Nether. For longer trips, use the Coordinator to set a destination—another dimension and coordinates, a biome, or a structure. Main doors and sub-doors come in pairs for easy return trips.

Crafting the door requires a dragon head. Craft the Coordinator afterward to decide where the door truly goes.
